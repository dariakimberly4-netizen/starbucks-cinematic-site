FOLLOW THE STORY
CHAPTER 01 · THE BEGINNING

A SMALL STORE. A BIG IDEA.

Starbucks began as a single store in Seattle’s Pike Place Market, built around high-quality whole bean coffee and a deep respect for the craft behind every cup. Over time, that idea grew beyond coffee itself into a place for conversation, belonging, and human connection.

CHAPTER 02 · THE THIRD PLACE

BETWEEN HOME AND WORK.

The coffeehouse vision became something simple but powerful: a welcoming third place between home and work. A place to slow down, meet someone, share a thought, or simply sit with a cup and feel part of something larger.

THE STORY ARRIVES IN THE PHILIPPINES.

1997

On December 4, Starbucks opened its first Philippine store at the 6750 Ayala Building in Makati City.

3rd

The Philippines became Starbucks’ third market outside North America—an important milestone in the brand’s international journey.

Connection

Warm hospitality, a love for coffee, and a natural desire for community helped Starbucks and Filipino coffee culture fit together.

MORE THAN JUST COFFEE

EVERY CUP CAN BECOME A CONNECTION.

Starbucks describes its mission around every cup, every conversation, and every community—nurturing the possibilities of human connection.

CHAPTER 03 · THE CRAFT

FROM BEAN TO CUP.

Quality remains central to the Starbucks story—from sourcing coffee to roasting with care and preparing beverages by hand. The craft is not presented as an ending, but as the beginning of what happens next: a moment shared between people.
